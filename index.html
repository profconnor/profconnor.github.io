Dylan Connor is an Associate Professor in the School of Geographical Sciences and Urban Planning at Arizona State University and Director of the Life Chances Lab.

He develops large-scale population data infrastructures to study the geography of life chances: how do our changing neighborhoods and regions dictate our ability to be upwardly mobile, build wealth, and live long, healthy lives?

Learn more about the Geography of Life Chances and the People-Experience-Places (PEP) Framework.
